# Setup

## Order

1. Install Grok Bot
2. Create Atlas
3. Paste `charters/atlas.md`
4. Create Scout, Quill, Pitch, Vault, Ledger
5. Paste each charter
6. Create outcome chats
7. Add Atlas to every chat
8. Add the specialist who owns that outcome
9. Record one full workflow
10. Give Atlas the first outcome

## First outcome to give Atlas

Use this sentence:

"Stand up the floor for this week.
I want verified prospects, queued outreach, draft content in my voice,
inbox at zero by 9am, and one number I should care about tomorrow.
Do not publish. Do not send. Do not spend. Ask me only when a decision is irreversible."

## How to teach

Record your screen once:

- how you qualify a prospect
- how you write a first touch
- how you sort inbox
- what "done" looks like for a content draft
- what number Ledger is allowed to pick

Talk while you do it.
Do not write a novel after the recording.

## What "done" means on day 1

Atlas posts:

- morning plan
- nightly shipped list

You approve:

- outreach drafts
- anything that spends money
- anything that goes public
