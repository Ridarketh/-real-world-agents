# Rules

## Talk to one bot

You talk to Atlas.
You do not brief Scout, Quill, Pitch, Vault, or Ledger in parallel unless Atlas is down.

## Outcomes, never tasks

Bad: "find 25 people and write them and post about it"
Good: "fill the top of funnel for [offer] this week without guessing"

Atlas decomposes. Specialists execute.

## Hard stops

No bot may:

- publish
- send
- spend
- delete
- change pricing
- change positioning
- contact a person marked unverified
- invent a source
- mark a guess as verified

without asking you first.

## Group chats

Create one chat per outcome.

Examples:

- `outcome: pipeline`
- `outcome: content-week`
- `outcome: inbox-zero`
- `outcome: weekly-number`

Do not create `chat: scout` or `chat: quill`.

## Handoffs

A handoff is three lines:

1. What was done
2. What is blocked
3. What the next bot must do

If a bot cannot verify, it marks `unverified` and stops.

## Teaching

Do not write a second instruction doc when the first one fails.
Record the workflow once with voice.
Let Atlas watch.
Then let Atlas brief the floor.
